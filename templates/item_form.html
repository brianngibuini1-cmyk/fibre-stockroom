How stock moves

  • Receive stock

Cable & serials

Cable is counted in whole drums, so quantities stay simple. Add a typical metres-per-drum value for an approximate length on hand, and record the real metres on receipts or issues when drum lengths differ.

Low-stock rule

An item is flagged as low stock when its on-hand quantity is at or below the reorder level. Set the reorder level to the point where you would normally restock.
